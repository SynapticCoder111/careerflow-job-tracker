FAQ

Questions, answered honestly

CareerFlow is a personal job application tracker. You record each role you apply to, move it through stages, schedule interviews and follow-ups, and see how your search is progressing. It is a tracking and organisation tool — it does not apply to jobs on your behalf.

Students and graduates managing campus drives and off-campus applications, and experienced professionals juggling several opportunities at once. The pipeline view suits people with many live applications; the analytics suit those who want to know which sources actually work.

Yes. CareerFlow records where you found a role — LinkedIn, a company site, a referral, campus placement, a job portal, or elsewhere — and lets you filter and analyse by source. It does not scrape or sync with job boards; you add applications yourself.

Yes. Each application can hold multiple interview rounds, each with its own date, type, interviewer, preparation checklist, notes and outcome. Follow-up dates, application deadlines and assessment deadlines all appear in a combined agenda, with anything overdue listed first.

No. CareerFlow does not submit applications, message recruiters, or automate anything on external job boards. You apply as you normally would, then record the application here so you can track it. If that changes, it will be stated plainly on this page.

Yes — your applications are private to your account, so signing in keeps your workspace separate from anyone else's. You can explore this page and the interactive demo without one, and you can delete your account and all of its data at any time from Settings.

Yes. Settings lets you export everything as a CSV file, download a full JSON backup, or import a CSV you already have. Imported rows are validated one at a time, and any rejected rows are listed so nothing disappears silently.
